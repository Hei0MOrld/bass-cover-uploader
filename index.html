Bass Cover Uploader

A personal tool by an individual bassist.

Bass Cover Uploader is a small desktop tool that the developer uses on his own PC to publish his own bass cover performance videos. After a recording session, it cuts a vertical short version of the video and, after the developer reviews it and presses a button, uploads it to the developer's own accounts (YouTube, Instagram, X and TikTok).

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users. It does not collect data from anyone else.
