Privacy Policy

Bass Cover Uploader · Last updated: September 25, 2026

Who uses this app

Bass Cover Uploader is a personal tool used only by its developer, on the developer's own computer, to upload the developer's own performance videos to the developer's own social media accounts. It has no other users.

Information the app accesses

TikTok

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar) — used only to confirm that the connected account is the developer's own account before uploading.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish) — used only to upload videos that the developer has recorded and chosen to post.

The app does not read followers, messages, comments, analytics or any content of other users.

How information is stored

Access tokens issued by TikTok are stored only in a local file on the developer's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the developer or by third parties. Video files stay on the developer's computer and are sent only to the platform they are being posted to.

Sharing

No information is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

The developer can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services). Deleting the local token file removes all stored access information.

Contact

Questions about this policy: GitHub Issues.

Bass Cover Uploader は、開発者本人が自分のPCで、自分で録画した演奏動画を自分のSNSアカウントへ投稿するためだけに 使う個人用ツールです。本人以外の利用者はいません。

共有: 情報の販売・第三者提供・広告や分析への利用は行いません。
